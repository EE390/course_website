What this is, and what it is not

  • The assembler accepts the same syntax as MIDE-51 (ASEM-51). Every example on this site assembles here to exactly the same bytes as MIDE-51 produces, and that is checked automatically whenever the site changes.
  • The processor model is the one used to verify the course solutions: full instruction set, Timer 0 and Timer 1 in modes 1 and 2, interrupts with priority, and 12T timing at 11.0592 MHz.
  • Timing here is simulated time, not your computer's clock. The reading under “Board” is what the chip would have experienced.
  • Not simulated: the LCD, I²C EEPROM, DS1302 clock, ADC, temperature sensor, IR receiver and the serial port. Programs using those still assemble, but their pins just move.
  • A working program here should behave the same on the real board. If it does not, trust the board, and tell us: report it.
